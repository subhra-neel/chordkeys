Help, troubleshooting and backup

Reading the screen


  • Amber is the right hand, teal is the left hand, green is a key you are pressing.
    • 

  • Numbers are fingers: 1 is the thumb, 5 is the little finger. Chord names are above the staff. Red notes are outside your keyboard.
    • 

  • Sargam: choose Sargam or Both in the note-naming menu. Capitals are shuddha (S R G M P D N), lower case is komal (r g d n), and M̀ with a ` on top is tivra Ma. A dot below is mandra saptak (low), no dot is madhya, a dot above is taar (high). Madhya Sa is the Sa nearest middle C. Sa follows the key of the piece, or lock it with the Sa = menu. Turn on Note names to label every key, not just Sa.
    • 

  • Tap the staff to jump. Space plays or pauses, the arrow keys step through chords, R restarts and M switches the metronome (when you use a physical keyboard).


Getting a score or chords


MuseScore: on musescore.com download the MIDI or MusicXML version. In the MuseScore app use File, Export. Open the file with Open file, or drag it onto this page. MusicXML keeps repeats as written once, and any chord symbols in the score become the chord names.
Songsterr: its files are Guitar Pro. Open them in MuseScore Studio (free), export MIDI or MusicXML, then open that here. Or copy the chords into Type chords.
Ultimate Guitar: copy the chord chart and paste it into Type chords. You can also save it as a .txt file and open it. Capo, tuning and lyrics lines are skipped. A chart is a guide, so check the bars in the preview.
PDF scores cannot be read, because they are pictures of the music.


Keyboard not detected?


  • Use Chrome, Edge or Opera. Safari and iPad browsers cannot use USB MIDI.
  • Plug the keyboard in first, then open or reload the app. Use an OTG adapter on a tablet.
  • When the browser asks to use MIDI devices, choose Allow. If you blocked it, change it in the site settings.
  • Prefer USB to Bluetooth: Bluetooth adds delay that makes your timing look late.


No sound?


  • Most keyboards make their own sound. To hear notes from the app, set Sound my notes to on in the settings.
  • Raise the tablet volume and tap the screen once so the browser allows audio.


Your data


Everything stays on this device. Nothing is uploaded. Clearing the browser data erases your progress, so back it up now and then.
