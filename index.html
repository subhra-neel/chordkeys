Help, troubleshooting and backup

Reading the screen

  • Amber is the right hand, teal is the left hand, green is a key you are pressing.
  • Numbers are fingers: 1 is the thumb, 5 is the little finger. Chord names are above the staff. Red notes are outside your keyboard.
  • Tap the staff to jump. Space plays or pauses, the arrow keys step through chords, R restarts and M switches the metronome (when you use a physical keyboard).

Getting a MIDI file

In MuseScore choose File, Export, then MIDI. For the best chord names export just the piano part. You can also drag a .mid file onto this page.

Keyboard not detected?

  • Use Chrome, Edge or Opera. Safari and iPad browsers cannot use USB MIDI.
  • Plug the keyboard in first, then open or reload the app. Use an OTG adapter on a tablet.
  • When the browser asks to use MIDI devices, choose Allow. If you blocked it, change it in the site settings.
  • Prefer USB to Bluetooth: Bluetooth adds delay that makes your timing look late.

No sound?

  • Most keyboards make their own sound. To hear notes from the app, set Sound my notes to on in the settings.
  • Raise the tablet volume and tap the screen once so the browser allows audio.

Your data

Everything stays on this device. Nothing is uploaded. Clearing the browser data erases your progress, so back it up now and then.
